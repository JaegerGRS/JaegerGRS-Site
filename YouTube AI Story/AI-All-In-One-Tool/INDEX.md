## ⚠️ RISKS & MITIGATION

### Risk 1: YouTube Demonetization
**If:** Your channel violates YouTube policies (copyright, spam, etc.)  
**Impact:** Lost revenue for months, possible channel deletion  
**Mitigation:** Aggressive plagiarism detection (built into tool), proper licensing, diverse content

### Risk 2: Niche Saturation
**If:** Your niche is oversaturated (everyone making same content)  
**Impact:** Low view growth, can't break through  
**Mitigation:** Test multiple niches (month 5-8), pivot fast to what wins, find unique angle

### Risk 3: Algorithm Changes
**If:** YouTube changes recommendation algorithm  
**Impact:** Overnight drop in views/subs (happened before)  
**Mitigation:** Diversify content, stay consistent, adapt quickly, don't rely on one video type

### Risk 4: Tool Breaks
**If:** AI models change, API errors, GPU crashes  
**Impact:** Can't create content for days  
**Mitigation:** Fallback options, local model backups, error monitoring, manual override

### Risk 5: Burnout (Even Automated)
**If:** Managing channel gets tedious  
**Impact:** Stop uploading, audience leaves  
**Mitigation:** Automate everything (upload schedule), take breaks, hire help year 2+
