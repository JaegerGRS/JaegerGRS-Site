## VIRAL SHORTS FORMULA

### The 3-Second Hook
First 3 seconds MUST stop the scroll:
- "Wait, did you know..."
- "Stop doing this wrong"
- "This changes everything"
- "Nobody talks about this"
- "I can't believe this works"

### The Body (Seconds 4-50)
- Fast cuts every 2-3 seconds
- Caption everything (80% watch without sound)
- Show, don't just tell
- Use trending sounds (if appropriate)
- Add curiosity gaps

### The CTA (Last 5 Seconds)
- "Follow for part 2"
- "Comment if you knew this"
- "Save this for later"
- "Which one surprised you?"
- "Subscribe for more"

### Retention Tricks
- Pattern interrupts (sudden sound/visual change)
- "But wait..." transitions
- Numbered lists (people want to see all items)
- Open loops (promise payoff at end)

## LONGFORM STRATEGY (10-20 min videos)

### When to Start Longform
- After you have 10k+ subscribers
- Once you know your winning Shorts topics
- When you can sustain 50%+ retention

### Longform Topics That Work
- Deep dives into viral Shorts topics
- "Complete guide to..."
- Storytime videos
- Tutorials with screen recording
- Compilation videos (your best Shorts)

### Longform Structure
```
0:00 - 0:30  Hook (promise value)
0:30 - 2:00  Intro + what they'll learn
2:00 - 15:00 Main content (chapters)
15:00 - 17:00 Examples/proof
17:00 - 18:30 Recap
18:30 - 19:30 CTA (subscribe, watch next)
```
