Method

How I diagnose a fault.

  1. Describe the symptom exactly. When it happens, how often, and what changed beforehand.
  2. Check the cheap things first. Seating, power and cables, then temperatures and event logs.
  3. Change one variable at a time. Swap or remove one thing, retest, and write down the result.
  4. Isolate the layer. Hardware, firmware, driver, operating system or application.
  5. Fix, then confirm. Repeat the original failing scenario.

Verification

How I confirm it worked.

  • Reproduce the original symptom before the change, so there is something to compare against.
  • Repeat the same scenario afterwards, under load where heat or stability was the issue.
  • Record temperatures and behaviour before and after, rather than relying on feel.
  • Keep notes of what changed, so a regression can be traced.
