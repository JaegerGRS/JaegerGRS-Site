Approach

Measure twice.
Change once.

01

Understand the constraint

What is failing, what actually matters, and what cannot be broken in the process?

02

Make the smallest useful change

Prefer clarity and maintainability over another layer of complexity.

03

Verify it behaves

Test the outcome, check what changed and document it for next time.
