Questions

Tool questions.

What do the status labels mean?

In development and Building now mean work has started (Building now is the same as In development). Planned means it is on the roadmap. The labels mirror svartsecurity.org and may change before launch.

Is anything collected about me?

That is the stated policy: no tracking, no ads and no telemetry, analytics or data collection. It is a policy statement and has not been independently audited.

How is data protected?

The design uses AES-256-GCM, with data encrypted on the device before it leaves. Treat this as the plan for each tool until it is released.
