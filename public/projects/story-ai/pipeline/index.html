YouTube AI Story · The pipeline

Idea to video.

The stages a story goes through, from the first idea to a finished video file.

Stages

The intended six steps.

  1. Research: looks at trends so ideas are based on what people actually watch.
  2. Idea: generates unique story ideas for a chosen niche or audience.
  3. Script: turns the idea into a script that reads like a person wrote it, with emotion markers.
  4. Voice: creates the voiceover from a saved character, using local text-to-speech.
  5. Render: builds the video using the CPU, RAM and GPU, as a short or a long-form piece.
  6. Check: runs an originality check and saves a record of how the video was made.

Design choices

Local, light and honest.

Heavy models only load when a video job starts, so the app stays light while idle. Everything runs on my own machine.

Each project keeps a local manifest with file hashes, so there is a record of how it was created. The originality checker works in several layers, and is there to help ensure ideas are inspired, not copied. It is a safeguard, not a guarantee, and the tool is still in development, so not every stage is finished.
