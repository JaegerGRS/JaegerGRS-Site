Overview

Story content, made faster.

The idea is simple: take a story from idea to finished video with as much help from AI as is sensible, while keeping it original and keeping me in control of the result.

It is built to make both short clips and longer videos, and it is being moved into a lightweight Tauri desktop app, the same approach I use for JAE.

About this project.

Is it finished?

No. It is in development: the pipeline modules exist and the desktop app is being built. I will update this page as it moves forward.

Is the content original?

That is the aim. It includes a plagiarism checker so ideas can be inspired by trends without copying anyone.

Does it run in the cloud?

No. It is local-first and runs on my own PC.
