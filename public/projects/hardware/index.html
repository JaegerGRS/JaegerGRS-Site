Overview

Where it began.

PC building is where my technical work began at 16, in 2016, when I got my own PC. I have hands-on experience selecting components, assembling machines, configuring Windows and investigating performance and stability problems.

Most of this experience comes from independently maintained systems and personal builds, documented as practical technical work.
