I'm autistic and I prefer written communication and remote or hybrid work, so a message here is the best way to reach me. I aim to reply by email.

Privacy, in short. The site has no message database. Your name, email address, topic and message are processed by the Worker and sent as an email to my inbox, where they stay until I delete them. The email also includes the country Cloudflare detects for your connection. To limit spam, the Worker keeps a salted, shortened hash of your IP address and the times of recent messages for up to 24 hours. It does not keep the IP address itself. Cloudflare, which hosts the site and sends the email, and my email provider handle this data too, and Cloudflare keeps request logs whose retention I have not configured. I use your details only to reply to you.

All fields are required. The topic has a default.
