About me

Learn by building.
Improve by testing.

I'm Jaeger Stratton, based in Peterborough, UK, with a hands-on background in PCs, systems and web development.

Get in touch ↗My journey

I'm 26, and IT has been half my life. I was introduced to tech at 13, started building websites in school at 14, and ran a YouTube channel on the side. At 15 I was researching more and more, and at 16 I got my own PC and started building computers.

I started out following tutorials, then gradually learned how to diagnose faults, compare components and work through problems myself.

That interest grew into websites and applications. I now build and maintain independent projects: privacy tools with Svart Security, a gaming channel and self-hosted site with Grey Signal, and developer work through JaeTheTech and JAE.

None of it came from a formal employer or a classroom. I've never been good in classroom environments, so I learn by building, testing and documenting. That's also why I'm looking for hybrid or remote work. I'm interested in IT support, PC assembly and technician roles, and open to opportunities beyond the UK.
